Prompt V1: conservative span selection

Additional annotation guidance:
- Mark a span only when the selected words themselves contain inflammatory language or persuasive propaganda.
- Do not annotate ordinary descriptions of political conflict, lawsuits, investigations, campaign strategy, public criticism, or policy disagreement unless the exact wording is loaded.
- Prefer short, exact spans over whole sentences.
- Do not label a phrase just because the surrounding article is political or controversial.
- When deciding between a polarizing label and No Polarizing language, choose No Polarizing language unless the span clearly matches a defined subcategory.

Hard negatives:
- Do not mark neutral reporting verbs such as "said", "criticized", "argued", "claimed", "responded", "reported", "asked", "called for", or "lashed out" by themselves.
- Do not mark factual descriptions of who did what unless the wording itself is emotionally loaded.
- Do not mark a quote simply because it comes from a partisan actor.
